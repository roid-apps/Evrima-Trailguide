HEALTH INDICATOR
Feature preparation passed 363 headless minimap checks, including compact
layouts, map-only mode, observation freshness, High Contrast and live palette
changes. Health uses matched visible Status Report evidence, without a new
capture loop, game-memory access or input interception. Unknown or conflicting

a claim of 100% health. Readings become stale after eight seconds.

offline screenshot OCR verify handling, not actual health for every dinosaur.

LIMITS
Client references are not live-server combat measurements. Fracture counts
assume qualifying registered contacts and no healing or parry; direct health
damage may kill first. Bleed estimates depend on the selected duration and
conditions, not a complete simulation of combat timing, needs and recovery.
Unselected effects, clogging and unqualified ability-specific paths are not
silently included. Ordinary attack references assume an intact attacker head.
No new live-game combat, physical hotkey or cross-process click-through test
was performed for this release. Offscreen renders do not activate windows,
register game hotkeys or perform live OCR. Tracking still requires visible
Status Report coordinates; routes use reference zones, not live server flags.
