**Make the island feel familiar.** Evrima Trailguide brings your map, Prime checklist, dinosaur references and run history into one Windows companion. Mark your next stop, compare a matchup, and take a compact mini-map back into the game.

Free to use. No account. Your screen readings and saved runs stay on your PC.

## Your map, your way

| A compact companion | Just the essentials |
| --- | --- |
| ![Actual compact mini-map with a sample destination and stale-reading label](images/v1.26.0/mini-map-compact.png) | ![Actual map-only mini-map with sample waypoint guidance](images/v1.26.0/mini-map-only.png) |

- **Keep the map close:** compact, exploration and passive map-only views, with follow/resume-follow controls.
- **Make it readable:** terrain opacity, labels, marker size and colour, breadcrumb styling and map layers.
- **Plan the route:** named waypoints, navigation targets, ordered stops and shareable route records.
- **Keep your setup:** save up to eight personal mini-map layouts.

Trailguide reads **your own visible Status Report coordinates**. Open that screen to refresh; when the numbers are hidden, the map retains the last accepted fix and shows its age. It does not reveal other players or read game memory. Borderless/windowed play is intended.

## Start your first run

1. **Install:** [download stable 1.26.0](https://github.com/roid-apps/Evrima-Trailguide/releases/download/v1.26.0/EvrimaTrailguide-Setup-1.26.0.exe). Close Trailguide before upgrading; keep a profile backup.
2. **Set up:** open **Setup check**, then Evrima’s **Status Report**. Choose **Scan setup → Auto setup** and inspect the capture areas.
3. **Explore:** start scanning, confirm a fresh reading and select **Show mini-map**. Hover the game’s growth bar to reveal the precise percentage.

**Stable 1.26.0 shortcuts:** `Ctrl+Shift+M` show/hide · `Ctrl+Shift+I` mouse interaction · `Ctrl+Shift+H` map-only. Customize them in Overlay options.

[Full walkthrough](https://youtu.be/U__SdPqlwzQ) · [Quick introduction](https://youtube.com/shorts/b4B2yn6n7Ws) · [1.26.0 release notes](https://github.com/roid-apps/Evrima-Trailguide/releases/tag/v1.26.0)

The videos introduce the core workflow and predate Glacier.
