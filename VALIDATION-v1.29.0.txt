Preset tests verify the strict versioned 64 KiB format, bounded presentation

and readability changes update it without replacing a pending imported look.

Window listeners detach on close and ignore queued post-close refreshes.

LINEAGE

generations. Missing, conflicting or legacy records stay separate. Current
and archived lives are deduplicated by recorded identity. The tree does not
