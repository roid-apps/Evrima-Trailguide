LIMITS
- A fresh live-game session under GPU load, physical mixed-DPI/monitor changes,
  clean-machine installation and extended-session performance qualification
  were not completed in the final review.
- OCR requires the relevant game text to be visible. Suspicious coordinate
  changes retain corroboration and false-teleport guards. Native OCR which
  never acknowledges cancellation may still require restarting the app.
- Status is processed before growth on the auxiliary worker; a failing status
  operation may defer growth. Coordinates have a separate worker.
- Mini-map guidance is north-up/map-relative, not dinosaur-facing heading,
  hidden-player detection or terrain-aware pathfinding.
- Combat, Prime and growth outputs retain their stated reference assumptions.
  No game-balance changes or new server-wide accuracy certification is claimed.
- Promotional screenshots are actual app renders using isolated test profiles.
  Portraits are AI-assisted model-referenced illustrations, not official game
  screenshots, exact models or growth measurements.
- Installer is unsigned. No Microsoft endorsement or new analyst certification
  is claimed. SmartScreen reputation warnings are not themselves malware findings.
