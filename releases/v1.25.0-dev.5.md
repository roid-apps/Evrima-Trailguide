**Map-only now automatically enables click-through and blocks mouse activation**, including window edges. It stays passive when reopened, after hide/show and during display-recovery handling. An old interactive map-only preference is normalized safely.

Use **Ctrl+Shift+I** to restore interactive controls for moving/resizing. **Ctrl+Shift+M** shows/hides, **Ctrl+Shift+H** toggles map-only. Hotkeys are configurable; main-window recovery remains available.

## 2. Combat: bring the dinosaurs to the forefront

![Visual combat comparison](https://github.com/roid-apps/Evrima-Trailguide/releases/download/v1.25.0-dev.5/combat-visual.png)

- Growth-viewer silhouettes now follow each combatant's species and growth slider.
- Separate **Compare dinosaurs** and **Attack calculator** modes, with visible inputs and pinned result summaries.
- Independent mutation loadouts, health-proxy and condition controls.
- Direct-hit comparisons separated from bleed estimates; manual blood reserves and optional experimental growth capacity.
- Improved growth/attack references and explicit handling of unsupported abilities and missing data.
- Compact controls, full-width mutation editing and expandable assumptions/details.
- Fixed attack selection disappearing when switching species.

The tool estimates reference scenarios, **not who wins a live fight**. Weight-based health proxies are not measured HP. Illustration sizes are independent, not one shared ruler. Armor, timing, positioning, reflection and special effects are not comprehensively simulated; unsupported effects are not invented.

## 3. Expedition planner: more of your run in one place

Open **Trailguide → Expedition planner**.

- **Food & routes:** menus for 22 released-species references, nutrient filters, juvenile guidance, resource search/pins, resource-first Prime itineraries and separate personal diet observations.
- **Mutation workshop:** saved loadout plans, slots/acquisition notes, carried Entombs, manual progress and comparison with your recorded lineage. Planning does not silently equip mutations.
- **Recovery:** rest/move scenarios for health, blood, stamina and fracture using player-measured rates; food/water runway and manual hit-cadence comparisons.
- **Field notes:** expiring sightings, crossings, hazards, rest/cover notes, map pins and migration observations with freshness guards.
- **Family:** parent/partner notes, nest waypoints, offspring milestones and user-set reminders.
- **Rules & lineage journal:** separate Public/HordeTest/Custom contexts and manual stored-lineage records. Non-public context pauses automatic public-zone evidence.
- **Sharing & history:** offline phone-friendly route cards, sample-density views and context preserved in new archives/exports.
- **Field manual:** beginner workflow, species references and practical combat/bleed/diet/water/survival guidance.

![Measured-input recovery planner](https://github.com/roid-apps/Evrima-Trailguide/releases/download/v1.25.0-dev.5/expedition-recovery.png)

Reference locations do not detect spawned food. Itineraries are not terrain-aware pathfinding. Recovery uses your measured rates, not universal timers. The manual lineage journal does not save/restore a game character. No live sharing server, verified HordeTest data pack or personalized migration OCR is included.
