  cannot be tracked; the last accepted fix and its age remain visible.
- A 0.5-second scan interval is a polling preference, not a guaranteed update deadline.

  sign/digit error or implausible jump. History preserves gaps in unseen movement.

  character tracking session. Saved-image and automated tests do not replace that.
